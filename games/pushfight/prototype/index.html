Push Fight — Official Rules

Goal

Be the first to push one of your opponent's pieces off the edge of the board at either open end!

Components (Each Player)

  • 3 Square Pieces (Kings): Can slide orthogonally. The only pieces with the power to push!
  • 2 Round Pieces (Pawns): Can slide and be pushed, but cannot push.
  • 1 Shared Red Anchor: Placed on the King that just pushed. Protects that King from being pushed on the opponent's next turn.

Turn Sequence

  1. 1. Move Phase (Optional): Make up to 2 sliding moves. Slide any friendly piece any distance orthogonally through empty spaces (Rook movement). You can move 1 piece twice, 2 pieces once, or skip.
  2. 2. Push Phase (Mandatory): You must use one of your Square Kings to push an adjacent piece. A push shifts a contiguous line of pieces 1 square.

Push Restrictions

  • Cannot push into or across a Side Rail (the raised wooden barriers).
  • Cannot push any line containing the Anchored King.
  • Pushing off an Open Edge immediately pushes that piece off the board!
